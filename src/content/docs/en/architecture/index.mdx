> **Applicable version: technical preview.** P2P reachability, OpenWrt hardware, non-zero `SO_MARK`, long-running load, WAN/netem, and public NAT traversal are not universal support claims.

WireGuard encrypts locally before a bridge forwards datagrams to a carrier. The carrier prefers a direct P2P path and may fall back to STCP without exposing application plaintext.
