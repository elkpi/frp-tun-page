> **Applicable version: technical preview.** P2P reachability, OpenWrt hardware, non-zero `SO_MARK`, long-running load, WAN/netem, and public NAT traversal are not universal support claims.

![frp-tun node dual-plane architecture: network isolation, WireGuard overlay, bridge pump, and frp carrier underlay.](/images/diagrams/architecture-overview-en.svg)

frp-tun adopts a **Dual-Plane** design that strictly decouples end-to-end encryption from carrier transport:

1. **Isolation & Policy Routing Layer**: Installs guard blackhole routes before configuring interfaces to eliminate unencrypted packet leaks during failover or recovery;
2. **WireGuard Overlay Plane**: Encrypts datagrams at local loopback UDP ports before packets reach user-space carriers;
3. **Bridge Pipeline**: Pumps datagrams between WireGuard loopback sockets and active carriers, multiplexing probe frames via `ProbeMux`;
4. **Carrier Underlay Plane**: Prioritizes direct P2P (XTCP/QUIC) connections and seamlessly fails over to STCP relays under supervisor control.
